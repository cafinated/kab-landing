Friendships don’t fade from lack of love. They fade from logistics.

Nobody decides to stop seeing a friend. Plans die in six specific ways:

Nobody’s free together

Someone suggests Saturday. Two people are out of town. Repeat.

Nobody decides

Group chats optimise for not offending anyone, which means no decision.

One person does it all

Every group has an organiser. They burn out. The group stops meeting.

Nobody mentions budget

“That place is too expensive for me” goes unsaid. So people quietly drop out.

Same three restaurants

Great things happen in the city and your group never hears about them.

Slow drift

“It’s been a year.” You didn’t choose it. It just happened.

How it works

Three options, not thirty. We decide for you — the group just vetoes.

  1. 1

    Raise it

    Anyone says “let’s meet” in the group. Optional: “dinner, under ₹800.”

  2. 2

    We find the window

    Everyone replies with when they’re free. We find the slots that work for most.

  3. 3

    Three real options

    Place + time + price per head + travel time + weather + any live offer.

  4. 4

    Vote & lock

    One tap each. Majority wins, calendar invites go out, reminders follow.

What makes it different

  • Private budgets. Everyone quietly sets a comfortable range. We only show options that fit the lowest one. Nobody sees anyone’s number.
  • Answers, not a feed. Other apps show you everything and make you choose. We give you three.
  • Real availability. Not another poll nobody answers.
  • WhatsApp-native. Your friend who refuses to install anything can still vote.
  • Local and actionable. Venues nearby with live offers — a plan you can act on, not just agree to.

Our privacy promise: when calendar sync arrives it will be busy/free only — never event titles — and we’ll never sell your data. Right now we only need a few replies in a chat.

Questions

Is it an app?

Not yet. It starts as a WhatsApp experience so nobody has to install anything. An app comes later, if the groups using it want one.

What does it cost?

Free during early access. Later the core will stay free for groups; paid extras and venue partnerships keep the lights on. Users never pay for bookings.

Will it read my calendar or my chats?

No. Early access uses simple replies in the chat. Calendar sync, when it comes, is opt-in and busy/free only.

Why only Delhi NCR?

Good suggestions need local depth. I’d rather be great in a few neighbourhoods than mediocre everywhere.

Is this a way to meet strangers?

No. Kab. is for the friends you already have.
